# Shipping Policy

## Processing time

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2 pm, on weekends or on public holidays ship the next business day. Large items such as tents can take up to 2 business days to process.

## Shipping options and costs (United States)

- **Standard (3 to 6 business days):** $6.95, free on orders over $75.
- **Expedited (2 to 3 business days):** $14.95.
- **Overnight (next business day):** $29.95. Order by 12 pm Eastern Time.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia. International orders take 7 to 14 business days. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery and are not included in our prices.

## Tracking your order

You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first update.

## Lost or late packages

If your tracking hasn't updated for 5 business days, or the package shows as delivered but you can't find it, contact us. We open a claim with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, send a replacement or a full refund.

## Changing your address

We can change the shipping address only before the order ships. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number.
