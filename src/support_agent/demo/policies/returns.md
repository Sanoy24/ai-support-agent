# Returns and Refunds

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, clean and in their original packaging with tags attached.

## Items that can't be returned

- Worn footwear with dirt on the soles (try boots on indoors first)
- Opened fuel canisters
- Gift cards
- Items marked "Final sale"

## How to start a return

Contact support with your order number. We email you a prepaid return label. Pack the item, attach the label and drop it at any carrier location.

## Return shipping cost

Returns are free in the United States. For international orders, return shipping is paid by the customer.

## Exchanges

The fastest way to exchange an item is to return it for a refund and place a new order. That way the new item ships right away instead of waiting for the return to arrive.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive and inspect the return. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo. We send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, and you won't need to return the item.

## Warranty

All Trailhead Supply gear has a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear and tear, accidents and misuse aren't covered.
