# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I choose my size?

Our jackets and boots run true to size. If you're between sizes in a jacket, or you plan to layer a fleece underneath, size up. For boots, measure your foot in the evening and use the size chart on each product page.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Your card is charged when the order is placed.

## Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel an order until it has been shipped. Contact support with your order number. Once an order has shipped, you can return it instead.

## Do you offer discounts?

Students, military members and first responders get 10% off with verification at checkout. We don't price match other retailers.

## Do you have a physical store?

No. Trailhead Supply Co. is online only, which lets us keep prices low.

## How do I care for waterproof gear?

Wash waterproof jackets with a technical cleaner on a gentle cycle, then tumble dry on low heat to restore the water-repellent finish. Don't use fabric softener.

## How can I contact a person?

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm Eastern Time. Ask the assistant to connect you with a person and it will pass on your conversation so you don't have to repeat yourself.
